03 · Entry documentsVisas & Border Formalities

🇻🇳 Vietnam — e-Visa

  • Apply: evisa.gov.vn (official Immigration Dept portal)
  • Fee: $25 single entry · $50 multiple entry
  • Validity: up to 90 days, single or multiple entry — covers your whole stay
  • Processing: typically 3–5 working days (some same-week; fee non-refundable if rejected)
  • Entry port: Noi Bai International (HAN), Hanoi
  • Docs: passport bio scan + 4×6 cm photo (white bg, no glasses), hotel address, onward ticket
  • Carry: 2 printed colour copies + digital copy

🇱🇦 Laos — e-Visa or Visa on Arrival

  • Option A — e-Visa: laoevisa.gov.la (MOFA official) · Tourist T-B3 · $50 + ~$1.75 · 3 working days · valid at LPQ airport ✓
  • Option B — Visa on Arrival at Luang Prabang Airport: $40 exact cash USD + 1 passport photo + filled form; 15–30 min; +$1–2 "overtime" fee possible (your flight lands 14:15 — normal hours)
  • Both grant 30 days single entry — plenty for 9 nights
  • Before landing, fill the free LDIF arrival card online (QR given on aircraft/immigration desks)
  • Passport: 6+ months validity, 2 blank pages; carry hotel printout + your Vientiane–Doha onward ticket
  • Beware lookalike visa sites charging $90–130 — only the two official domains above
No other visas needed: your Hanoi→Luang Prabang flight is a simple international departure from HAN T2 + arrival at LPQ; the Vientiane→Doha flight departs Wattay. No Vietnam exit visa, no transit visas on any routing via BKK/CAN/SIN.

Rules verified against official portals, October 2026. If your passport is not on the e-Visa eligibility list, use embassy sticker visas — allow 2 weeks. Requirements are quoted for the common case (e.g., Indian/other passports needing both visas); visa-exempt nationalities (13 listed by Vietnam, incl. Japan, Korea, UK, EU Schengen states, etc. — 45-day Vietnam stay; several ASEAN passports) should still verify on the portals.

05 · The full day-by-dayItinerary, 4–20 December 2026

Day 0 · Fri 20 Oct → Fri 3 Dec — Countdown (see Section 02)Home base: Doha

Today kicks off the booking sprint: Vietnam e-visa, HAN→LPQ flight, Ha Long cruise, Sapa night train, hotels — then Laos e-visa mid-November and the railway tickets in early December. By 3 Dec every voucher should be printed and your USD cash sorted.

Day 1 · Fri 4 Dec — Arrive Hanoi, 21:00Sleep: Old Quarter
21:00Land at Noi Bai International (HAN). Immigration: passport + printed e-visa (30–45 min at this hour), baggage, SIM/eSIM already active.
21:50Transfer to Old Quarter: Grab app (cheapest, ~400,000 VND) or official airport taxi queue (~450,000 VND). 45–60 min night drive through Hanoi's ring roads. Pre-book a hotel pickup (~$18–25) if you prefer zero hassle after the long-haul.
23:00Check in (pre-arranged late check-in; most Old Quarter hotels hold your room all night). Drop bags, change out of travel clothes.
23:15Late dinner — you land hungry. Walk to Phở Gia Truyền (49 Bát Đàn) if still serving, or any 24h phở stall around Tô Tịch / Hàng Bài: steaming bowl of phở bò (beef phở) or bún chả if open. Alternatively hotel-area banh mi + iced coffee.
Hanoi in December is 14–22°C at night — bring a light jacket out of your daypack, not from the suitcase.
Day 2 · Sat 5 Dec — Hanoi Grand TourSleep: Hanoi · Meals: street food
07:00Breakfast: bánh cuốn (steamed rice rolls) at 14 Hàng Gà, or bún riêu crab noodle soup. Coffee: egg coffee (cà phê trứng) at Café Giảng (39 Nguyễn Hữu Huân) — the original.
08:30Hoàn Kiếm Lake & Ngọc Sơn Temple — cross the red Huc Bridge to the island temple; 30 min. Walk the lake's northern edge via Hàng Đào.
09:30Temple of Literature (Văn Miếu) — Vietnam's first university, 1070 AD; serene courtyards. ~30 min by foot/cyclo, 30,000 VND entry. (Closed Mon & Fri mornings for ceremonies sometimes — today is Saturday ✓.)
11:00Ho Chi Minh Complex — Mausoleum (closed Mon & Fri; Sat ok — queue by 10:45, no photos inside, shorts forbidden), One-Pillar Pagoda, Presidential Palace grounds, Ho Chi Minh Museum. Free entry, passport required. Dress shoulders/knees covered.
13:00Lunch: bún chả (grill-your-own pork patties + noodles) at Bún Chả Hương Liên (24 Lê Văn Hưu — the Obama-bun cha place) or chả cá fish hotpot on Chả Cá street.
14:30Old Quarter lanes walk — 36 streets each named after a trade (Hàng Bạc silver, Hàng Gai silk). St. Joseph's Cathedral, Đồng Xuân market, water-puppet craft shops.
16:00Hanoi Train Street — the famous narrow alley on Nguyễn Công Trứ / Phung Hung where the train passes between houses. Go late afternoon for the 15:15/17:00-ish passes; buy a café seat (coffee ~40,000 VND) and stand clear when the horn sounds. (Access rules change — if barriers block the classic spot, the Phung Hung mural street cafés still have views.)
18:00Water puppet show — Thăng Long Water Puppet Theatre, 57B Đinh Tiên Hoàng, tickets ~100,000 VND, shows hourly 18:00–21:00. 50 min of lacquered ducks, dragons and folk music on the pond stage.
19:30Dinner crawl: bánh xèo sizzling crepes (172 Đội Cấn), phở gà at Phở Thìn 13 Lò Đúc, then bia hơi (draft beer) at Bia Hoi Junction (Tạ Hiện) — the world's cheapest fresh beer, ~10,000 VND a glass.
Budget the day: entries ~150,000 VND + food ~250,000 VND + cyclo/Grab ~100,000 VND ≈ $20 pp.
Day 3 · Sun 6 Dec — Ninh Binh Day Trip ("Ha Long Bay on land")Sleep: Hanoi · Booked tour
08:00Pick-up from Old Quarter hotel by minivan (~2.5 h drive, 100 km south; comfort stop en route).
10:30Tràng An — sampan rowed by locals through karst caves and flooded valleys (2 h loop, three grottoes, ~200,000 VND entry incl. boat). The Dec light on the lime cliffs is superb. Sit left for cave entries.
12:30Lunch — included in most tours: goat meat (dê), crispy rice, local greens at a Tam Coc / Ninh Binh village restaurant.
13:30Mua Cave (Hang Múa) — 500 stone steps to the ridge viewpoint over Tam Coc's rice paddies and river: the iconic photo of the trip. Water + shoes with grip.
15:00Hoa Lu ancient capital — 10th-century temples of kings Đinh & Lê, set against cliffs. Short walk, 30 min.
16:00Optional Bai Dinh Pagoda — Vietnam's largest Buddhist complex (electric car ride across the hillside).
18:30Back in Hanoi. Dinner: chả cá or a bowl of bún bò Nam Bộ near your hotel. Early night — cruise pickup is early.
DIY version: bus from Giáp Nhập station to Ninh Bình (~2 h, 120,000 VND), then hire a motorbike driver locally for the loop (~300,000–500,000 VND/day). Tour is easier and only marginally pricier.
Day 4 · Mon 7 Dec — Ha Long Bay Overnight CruiseSleep: On board
07:30Hotel pick-up (Old Quarter meeting points 07:15–08:00) — limousine/minivan ~3.5 h via the new expressway to Tuan Chau Marina.
11:30Arrive marina, check-in lounge, then tender to your junk. Welcome drink, cabin assignment (book a sea-view balcony cabin if budget allows).
12:30Lunch onboard as the boat cruises among 1,600+ limestone islands. Watch for Dog Stone Islet, Fighting Cock Islet.
14:30Sung Sot (Surprise) Cave — the bay's largest grotto; cathedral chambers of stalactites (climb ~100 steps). Then Titop Island: swim off the tiny beach or climb 420 steps to the 360° panorama viewpoint.
17:00Sunset party + Vietnamese cooking class on the sundeck — learn fresh spring rolls as the sun drops behind karsts. Happy hour buy-2-get-1.
19:00Dinner onboard (seafood set menu), then squid fishing from the stern, stargazing, or bar. Anchored overnight in a quiet lagoon.
December Ha Long is often softly misty — atmospheric, not a cancellation. Operators sail unless official storms; kayak/ swimming runs if seas are calm.
Day 5 · Tue 8 Dec — Cruise Finale → Night Train to SapaSleep: Sleeper train SP15/SP1
06:00Tai chi on the sundeck; coffee as the mist lifts off the bay.
07:00Breakfast, then Luon Cave by kayak or bamboo boat through a hidden lagoon (monkeys on the walls). Some itineraries place Sung Sot/Titop on day 2 — either way you cover all three icons.
10:30Brunch while cruising back to Tuan Chau; disembark ~11:30.
12:00Limousine back to Hanoi — arrive ~15:30. Collect the pre-booked night-train tickets from your hotel/agent (or e-ticket + passport).
16:00Shower/refresh at hotel (use day-use room or late checkout booked for this date), last Hanoi stroll, pack daypack for Sapa. Leave the big suitcase locked at the hotel (most allow storage to 10 Dec).
19:45Dinner near station: phở or cơm (rice set). Taxi to Hanoi Railway Station (Ga Hà Nội, 120 Lê Duẩn).
21:45Board SP15 (or SP1 21:35 — whichever you booked). 4-berth soft-sleeper cabin, AC, bedding provided. Lights out early: 4-berth lower bunk is easiest with luggage. ~7 h 40 min.
Private tourist wagons (Chapa Express, Sapaly) attach to SP trains — wood-panelled cabins, bottled water, better linen: worth the $20 upgrade for a night you actually need to sleep on.
Day 6 · Wed 9 Dec — Sapa: Terraces & Hill TribesSleep: Sapa town
05:20Arrive Lao Cai station. Pre-booked shuttle/minivan up the 38 km mountain road to Sapa town (50–60 min, ~60,000–150,000 VND shared, or taxi 300,000 VND). Dawn over the Hoàng Liên Sơn foothills if skies clear.
06:30Early check-in or luggage room at hotel; hot shower + breakfast at the hotel (eggs, baguette — French colonial legacy — and strong local coffee).
08:30Start the classic valley trek with a local H'mong or Red Dao guide (~$25–40 for a private/guide-day, or free-ish if you simply walk): Cat Cat village (waterfall, traditional houses, 30 min downhill) — or the richer full-day Lao Chải → Tả Van walk along terrace ridgelines down to the Muong Hoa valley (~4–5 h, easy-moderate, rice terraces at their post-harvest golden stubble in December).
12:30Lunch in Tả Van village — bamboo-sticky rice, stream fish, thang co if you're adventurous (included on guided treks).
15:00Continue/return to Sapa town by jeep. Browse Sapa stone church and the weekend-adjacent market streets; try thắng cố or a warm hotpot for dinner.
19:00Early night — Sapa evenings in December drop to 5–10°C. Heated blanket + a rhum (corn whisky) nightcap.
Cold alert: Sapa Dec dawns can hit 4–8°C with fog or even frost on Fansipan. Layers, beanie, light gloves, proper shoes. Porters/guides can arrange rain ponchos cheaply.
Day 7 · Thu 10 Dec — Sapa Heights → HanoiSleep: Hanoi
07:00Choose your morning: Fansipan cable car to the 3,143 m "Roof of Indochina" summit (cable ~700,000 VND; go early for clear views above the cloud) — or the lighter Ham Rong flower garden walk right above town with valley panoramas.
11:00Check out. Board the limousine van Sapa → Hanoi (Sapa Express / Green Bus / Good Morning Sapa — reclining 9-seat vans, ~$18–25, hotel pick-up, 5–5.5 h, rest stop). Alternative: day train SP8 12:05 Lao Cai → 19:30 Hanoi if you prefer rails.
16:30Arrive Hanoi (hotel pick-up point). Reunite with the stored suitcase, laundry done, repack for Laos (lighter — LPB/VV/VTE are warm).
18:00Farewell-Vietnam evening: sunset drink at a rooftop bar (Hanoi Social Club / The Summit), then bún bò Huế or a final bún chả + banh mi corner feast. Pack carry-on for tomorrow's flight: documents, meds, swimwear (Kuang Si!), powerbank.
Hotels will often keep your big bag free if you rebook the 10th night with them — ask at check-in on the 4th.
Day 8 · Fri 11 Dec — Fly to Luang Prabang, Sunset on the MekongSleep: Luang Prabang
08:00Slow morning; breakfast; Grab to Noi Bai T2 (45 min — leave 3 h before departure for the international hop).
13:10VN931 Vietnam Airlines → Luang Prabang (1 h 05, A321; Lao Airlines QV314 19:10 is the backup). Departure from Hanoi T2.
14:15Land LPQ airport. Visa lane: e-Visa letter or VOA desk ($40 cash) — 15–30 min at this quiet airport, then luggage + customs.
15:15Transfer to hotel (4 km to the peninsula; tuk-tuk/taxi ~50,000–80,000 LAK or pre-booked). Check in to your guesthouse on the Mekong/Khan side.
16:00Old town orientation walk: Waten Street shophouses, Wat Visoun (Watermelon stupa), French colonial lanes. Snack: Lao coffee (pounded with a mortar — "coffee Lao") at a riverfront café.
17:15Mount Phousi (Phou Si) — 328 steps for the classic sunset over the Mekong and Nam Khan confluence, golden light on Luang Prabang's rooftops and distant Mekong bends. (Entry 20,000 LAK; go up Phousi, down the other side to the night market.)
18:30Night market on Sisavangvong Road — H'mong textiles, mulberry paper, riverweed crackers (kaipen). Dinner: barbecue street stalls — grilled fish in banana leaf, laap, sticky rice (khao niao) eaten with fingers.
You are now in Laos: kip (LAK) is the currency, ~21,000 = $1. ATMs on the main strip; USD cash widely accepted at better rates at banks/exchange shops.
Day 9 · Sat 12 Dec — Kuang Si Falls & Pak Ou CavesSleep: Luang Prabang · Full-day tour ~$35–50
08:00Minivan pick-up. First stop: Kuang Si Falls (29 km, 45 min) — tiered turquoise pools spilling 60 m through jungle. Swim in the lower pools (bring swimsuit), climb to the top viewpoint, and visit the Free the Bears rescue centre at the entrance (included).
11:30En route back: stop at a Khamu/Lao village and a rice-wine/whisky (lao-lao) tasting stall.
13:00Lunch in town (often included), then board a slow boat up the Mekong (~2 h upstream past sandbanks and fishing rafts).
15:30Pak Ou Caves — the river-cliff grottoes filled with thousands of small Buddha images left by pilgrims for centuries; two caves (lower: full of statues, upper: stalactites + a river view).
17:30Return boat + van to LPB. Dinner: buffet-Lao-style khantoke or orlam stew at a local kitchen on the Khan; or French-Lao bistro (Luang Prabang has superb French bakeries — croissants for tomorrow's alms-adjacent breakfast).
Kuang Si is a national park — no swimwear above the 3rd pool; pay the small photo/camera fee if asked (10,000 LAK). Waters are genuinely cold in Dec; a quick dip is bracing, not long.
Day 10 · Sun 13 Dec — Sacred Luang Prabang (Alms Day Sunday)Sleep: Luang Prabang
05:15Baci / quiet start — walk to Sakkaline Road in silence by 05:30 for binthabat (tak bat): the river of saffron-robed monks streaming past to collect sticky rice. Sunday is the main, longest procession. Buy a basket of rice (10,000–20,000 LAK) from a vendor; kneel, offer a small handful, don't touch the monks (women never touch). Watch only — no flash, no blocking.
06:30Breakfast; back to hotel for proper sleep or keep going.
08:30Royal Palace Museum (Haw Kham) — throne hall, the 16th-century Phra Bang Buddha image the city is named for, murals of Lao history. (Opens 08:00; dress respectfully.)
10:00Wat Xieng Thong — the masterpiece temple of Laos: sweeping tiered roofs, gold-tree mosaic on the rear wall, the funeral carriage tower. The single must-see monument of the city.
11:00Traditional Arts & Ethnology Centre (excellent small museum of hill-tribe textiles) or the Buffalo Dairy café for ethical elephant-free coffee & ice cream.
12:30Lunch: lao-lao sausage, laap, som tam (green papaya salad) at a riverside kitchen.
14:00Weaving & craft lanes: visit a H'mong weaving cooperative on the Khan (watch natural indigo & lotus-silk techniques), or take a short cooking class (market visit + 3 dishes, ~$30–45, ~3 h).
17:30Mekong sunset drink at a river-bar, then Bangkok-style street dinner: grilled meats, khao piak sen (Lao chicken noodle soup). Early night — train tomorrow.
Day 11 · Mon 14 Dec — Laos–China Railway to Vang ViengSleep: Vang Vieng
08:00Check out, tuk-tuk to Luang Prabang Railway Station (13 km, ~25 min, 40,000–60,000 LAK — station is well outside town, not walkable). Luggage through security; be seated 30–60 min early.
09:48Train C95 → Vang Vieng (Lane Xang EMU, 2nd class ~$11–15). 54 minutes of pure karst theatre — viaducts, tunnels, rice valleys; sit on the right (mountain) side heading south.
10:42Arrive Vang Vieng station (7 km from town). Songthaew/taxi to guesthouse (~20,000–30,000 LAK). Check in; leave bags.
12:00Lunch: riverside restaurant on the Nam Song — pizza/smoothies (the backpacker classics) or Lao set. Rent a bicycle (~30,000 LAK/day) for the afternoon.
13:30Blue Lagoon 3 (10 km west) — the prettiest of the three lagoons: milky-turquoise water in a jungle bowl, rope swings, a short walk to Tham Jung cave (climb for a sky-hole view). Entry ~10,000 LAK.
16:30Return; climb Tham Chang cave near town for the balcony view over the valley, or just drift on a bamboo tube in the river shallows.
17:45Sunset on the Nam Song — the limestone wall behind town goes amber. Beer Lao on a deck chair.
19:00Dinner: Vang Vieng main strip — try buffalo tartare (the local specialty) or happy-shake-free proper Lao food at a garden restaurant. Early night; tomorrow is a travel day but a short one.
Day 12 · Tue 15 Dec — Vang Vieng Morning → Train to VientianeSleep: Vientiane
07:30Morning on the river: choose one — tubing the Nam Song (float past caves and rope swings, ~2 h, ~55,000 LAK incl. tube), kayaking through the rapids section, or a bike ride to the rice paddies north of town with karst backdrop photos.
11:00Shower, check out, lunch near the strip. Book the songthaew to Vang Vieng station for ~14:45 (7 km).
15:52Train D87 → Vientiane (international CR200J Fuxing set, 2nd class ~$13). 52 minutes, then the Mekong plain opens up.
16:44Arrive Vientiane (Khamsavath) station. Tuk-tuk/taxi to hotel in the riverfront/old-town area (~20–30 min, 60,000–100,000 LAK). Check in.
18:00Mekong riverside promenade — the whole city walks here at dusk: street-food stalls, fruit shakes, the sun dropping toward Thailand across the river.
18:45Night market opposite the river (handicrafts, textiles) → dinner: khao soi luang prabang (minced-pork noodle soup — different from the Thai version), grilled chicken, sticky rice. Beer Lao dark at a riverside bar.
Day 13 · Wed 16 Dec — Vientiane Monument CircuitSleep: Vientiane
08:00Breakfast at hotel / jeow bong (buffalo-rind dip) with sticky rice at a local shop.
08:45Pha That Luang — Laos' holiest monument, a gold-plated stupa over a 16th-century relic; go early for empty courtyards and best light. Free / 30,000 LAK for the museum. Dress covered.
10:15Hai An / That Luang surroundings: walk to Patuxai Victory Monument (Laos' Arc de Triomphe — climb the interior stair for the city panorama, 5,000 LAK) through its weirdly wonderful mythological concrete reliefs.
11:30COPE Visitor Centre — the essential, sobering museum on UXO (unexploded ordnance) in Laos, with a reconstructed bomb-shelter home and prosthetics workshop. ~$2, 1.5 h. Tuk-tuk ~15 min from centre.
13:30Lunch: Lao-Italian or the excellent street food near Talat Sao (Morning Market); browse the market's textile floors upstairs.
15:00Wat Si Saket (thousands of tiny Buddholes in cloister niches) and Wat Ho Phra Keo (former royal temple, now a museum of sculpture).
18:00Cultural dinner: Kualao Restaurant (Samsenthai) — Lao set banquet with traditional dance show from ~19:00 (reserve ahead, ~$15–25 pp). Or French-Lao fine dining on the river.
Vientiane is compact — the monument circuit is 3–4 km total. Tuk-tuks quote 30,000–50,000 LAK per hop; agree the fare before hopping in. Grab coverage is thin; use hotel-arranged rides.
Day 14 · Thu 17 Dec — Buddha Park & Mekong Slow LifeSleep: Vientiane
08:30Buddha Park (Xieng Khuan) — 25 km east on the Mekong bank: a surreal 1958 sculpture field of 200+ Buddhist and Hindu figures — a giant reclining Buddha, a demonic "Hell Gate" pumpkin tower you climb through scenes of heaven and hell. Tuk-tuk round trip ~250,000–350,000 LAK (share with other guests), or local bus 44 from the Northern Bus Terminal, 5,000 LAK. Entry 15,000 LAK. ~2 h there.
11:30On the way back, stop at Nam Ngum Dam viewpoint area, or simply return to town.
13:00Lunch + Talat Sao deep-dive — the real market: coffee sacks, hill-tribe silver, silk sinh skirts, and the best Lao coffee beans (~50,000 LAK/250 g) to take home.
15:00Lao massage (oil or dry, 1 h ≈ 80,000–120,000 LAK) — the traditional style is firmer and more stretching than Thai; excellent after 14 days of walking.
17:00Sunset boat on the Mekong — short sampan cruise from the Chao Anouvong park jetty (~$8–12, 45 min) watching the Thai bank light up. Alternative: climb Phu Chai or the park hill… better: rooftop bar at your hotel with a Beer Lao.
19:00Dinner: orm-lam (smoky Lao lamb-rib stew) at a riverside restaurant, then a last wander of the promenade. Video-call home — the next two days are soft.
Day 15 · Fri 18 Dec — Slow Day: Rituals, Shopping, Reserve TimeSleep: Vientiane
06:30Optional: observe (not join) morning alms on the quiet lanes behind Wat Si Saket — smaller and more intimate than Luang Prabang's. Then coffee at Joma / Bean Café (Vientiane's best café culture).
09:30Reserve slot A: Full-day Nam Ngum Dam & island lunch trip (dam, reservoir boat, grilled fish lunch, ~$30–40 by charter/shared van) — OR — Reserve slot B: slow local day: That Dam (Black Stupa), French colonial Indochina-style villas on Rue Setthathirath, Axe Music/ bookshops, stationery and silk hunting.
13:00Lunch. Try ping kai (Lao grilled chicken) with jaew dipping sauces at a courtyard restaurant.
15:00Souvenir run: Lao coffee + whiskey-lao + sinh textiles + mulberry paper cards from Talat Sao or the Hmong market stalls on the riverfront. Ship nothing heavy — carry-on it home.
17:30Final Mekong sunset sit — the ritual of the trip: shake, sticky rice, watching the boats. Then pack properly for the flight (see Section 09 checklist).
Keep ~$50–80 in USD cash and enough kip for the airport taxi tomorrow; exchange leftover VND at banks (airport rates are poor).
Day 16 · Sat 19 Dec — Last Full Day in LaosSleep: Vientiane
09:00Luang Prabang-style indulgence, Vientiane edition: revisit your favourite café; or the Lao Textile Museum (hand-loomed silks, ~100,000 LAK); or a final 90-min massage.
11:00Pha That Luang revisited at golden hour of day — or the quiet alternative Simuong temple and the stupa's side lanes. Photos without crowds.
12:30Farewell lunch: sit-down Lao tasting menu — laap, tam mak hoong, orlam, khao niao, and a Lao coffee flan. Toast the trip.
14:30Packing & admin: final bag weigh-in, hand-wash pickup, e-ticket & passport photocopies, download offline maps for Doha arrival, message airline for meal/s seats if needed.
16:00Chao Anouvong Park stroll — riverfront gardens with views to Thailand; the fountain show at dusk if scheduled.
18:30Easy dinner near hotel: fried river fish, som tam, and an early night — especially if your Doha flight is morning; if it's evening, enjoy a longer final night.
Hotel tip: ask for late check-out (14:00) or day-use room for tomorrow; most Vientiane hotels are generous about storing luggage all day free.

09 · Field manualPractical Kit

🎒 Packing for this exact trip

  • Layer system for Hanoi/Sapa: base layers + mid fleece + windproof shell; beanie + gloves for Sapa dawn (4–8°C)
  • Light long-sleeves + trousers for temple visits (That Luang, mausoleum, Wat Xieng Thong)
  • Swimwear (Kuang Si, Titop, Vang Vieng lagoons) + quick-dry towel
  • Broken-in trekking shoes/sneakers + flip-flops for guesthouses
  • Power bank, universal adapter (VN types A/B/C; LA type C/E; both 220V)
  • Reusable water bottle (refill at hotels — plastic is a real problem in Laos)
  • Sun cream, insect repellent, basic meds (Imodium, rehydration salts, band-aids)
  • Passport photos ×4 (VOA backup), photocopies of passport + visas
  • Packable daypack for Sapa trek & day tours

📱 Connectivity & apps

  • eSIM: Airalo/Holafly regional Asia plan, or Viettel tourist SIM at HAN (~$10, best Sapa/Halong coverage) + Unitel at LPQ (~$5)
  • Grab — taxis & food in Vietnam; limited/no coverage in Laos (use hotel-arranged tuk-tuks)
  • Offline: Google Maps downloads for each city; Google Translate Lao + Vietnamese packs
  • Booking: Booking/Agoda (hotels), 12Go/Baolau (trains, buses), GetYourGuide (tours), Klook (Vietnam attractions)
  • Messenger/WhatsApp — default for tour operators in both countries

🩺 Health & safety

  • Travel insurance with medical evacuation — carry the policy number paper + app
  • Tap water: not for drinking; bottled/filtered is cheap everywhere (refill where possible)
  • Street-food rule: eat where queues of locals are; hot & freshly cooked = fine
  • Sapa trek poles help on the Mua Cave steps and terrace paths
  • Ha Long: use provided life jackets on kayaks/tenders; seas are calm in Dec
  • December fog on Sapa/Ha Long roads — book transfers with established operators
  • Check your routine vaccines are current (Hep A, typhoid recommended by most advisories)

☎ Numbers & etiquette

  • Emergency VN: police 113 · fire 114 · ambulance 115
  • Emergency LA: police 191 · ambulance 195 · fire 190
  • Tourist police Hanoi: 024 3939 1457 · Vietnam e-visa help on evisa.gov.vn
  • Temple etiquette: remove shoes, cover shoulders/knees, never point feet at Buddha, ask before photographing people
  • Alms in Luang Prabang: observe silently; don't stand over the monks, no flash, no touch (men only offer)
  • Bargain markets gently with a smile; round up fares kindly — small money matters more to them than you
  • Photography of military/police/border = never
